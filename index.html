Privacy & security

Everything in this app is stored only on this device, in this browser — it isn't shared with anyone (not even whoever sent you the link) and it never reaches a server. You can lock this copy with a password, which encrypts your data on the device.

There's no password reset. If you forget it, the encrypted data can't be recovered — keep a JSON backup (above) somewhere safe.
