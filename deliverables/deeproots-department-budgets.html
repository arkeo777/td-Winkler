Reality check before this goes to the board

The 4x goal is far above the current plan. The 2027 plan says "75 new schools" and "double sales." The July vision doc says "390 to 1,500+ schools in 18 months." This budget follows the 1,500-school path, which needs about new schools over two years. At 75 a year, that many schools would take about 15 years.

Biggest risks

  • Sales capacity. Reps said 20 new schools each per year is a stretch, and Julie is part-time. The plan adds inside sales and online ordering through the portal so reps aren't the only way to close a sale.
  • First-year deal size. A new school averages about $2,267 in year one. This plan assumes $3,000. Smaller first orders mean more schools are needed.
  • New markets don't exist yet. Homeschool, church, Spanish and high school must reach about $2.7M by year 2. Each one needs a launch owner and a date.
  • Year 1 cash. Spending runs ahead of sales in year 1. Donor money must at least stay at today's level until the year 2 sales arrive.
