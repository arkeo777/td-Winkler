# Winkler Quest — Catalog (draft v1)

Everything here is editable in the parent app. **Exchange rate: 10 coins ≈ $1.**
"Older" = the doer (Pinwheel). "Younger" = needs a push (iPhone).
Where the younger son's value differs, it's shown as (Y: …).

## How checking works (low-monitoring)
- **Photo in = done.** Chores with a photo count the moment the picture is uploaded.
  You get the photo as a notification; glance at it. Only if something's wrong, tap
  **Reject** (within 48 h) and the coins come back out.
- **Younger son, while he's building the habit:** big chores wait for one tap
  (Approve straight from the notification). As he proves himself, chores move to
  "photo in = done" automatically (see *Graduation* below).
- **Attitude buttons on every photo:** 😀 great attitude **+25%** · 😐 fine · 😠 grumbling **half pay**.

## Driver's license — Tennessee (older son)
Tennessee graduated license (verify current rules with TN Dept. of Safety):
- **Learner permit at 15**: written test; must hold it 180 days.
- **50 hours** of supervised driving, **10 at night**, certified by a parent.
- **Intermediate restricted license at 16**; unrestricted later with a clean record.

The app keeps the official-style drive log (date, minutes, day/night, supervising
parent), which is exactly what you have to certify — and every logged drive pays coins.
Map stages: 📘 Permit test passed → 🚗 10 h → 25 h → 🌙 10 night hours → 50 h →
🧾 Clean-record month → 🎉 License → **The Open Road** (car time).

## Making it last until there's real change
1. **Graduation.** When a habit is solid (e.g. 40 of 42 days for six weeks), it
   *graduates*: a badge on the map, it becomes "just what we do" (base pay drops),
   and its coins move to a new growth area. Rewards follow growth, not routine.
2. **Heart outweighs chores.** The biggest yearly prizes require the Heart meter
   (attitude, compliments, family joy, initiative), not just chore count.
3. **Initiative pays.** Doing it before a reminder earns more than doing it after.
4. **Seasons.** Every ~8 weeks a new map chapter, fresh goals and prizes, so the
   novelty doesn't wear off in month three.
5. **Monthly family check-in.** 15 minutes with the dashboard: what got better, each
   son picks one goal for next month.
6. **Change dashboard for parents:** trend lines for reminders needed, attitude
   score, initiative rate and photos rejected — the actual "is it working?" answer.
7. **Grace day.** One missed day per week doesn't break a streak — one bad day
   shouldn't wreck a month.
8. **Penalties stay small** next to rewards; the app is mostly about catching them doing good.
9. **Per-son difficulty.** Younger son starts with easy wins and the bar rises as
   his streaks grow; older son gets bigger, longer goals (license, cooking).

## Settings page (parents only)
Change values any time; changes apply **from now on** (coins already earned never change).

**Chores**: each chore shows a card:
- **Hardness:** 1–5 (Easy · Normal · Tough · Hard · Brutal) and **usual time** in minutes.
- **Suggested value** = minutes × 1 coin × hardness (×0.5 / ×1 / ×1.25 / ×1.5 / ×2),
  rounded to 5. Mowing (5 h, Hard) suggests 450; making a bed (3 min, Easy) suggests 5.
- **Value:** accept the suggestion, or nudge it with − / + (5 coins a tap).
- **Per son:** a separate value for each son if you want (e.g. coop pays the younger more).
- Also on the card: photo required? needs a tap to approve? who does it / taking turns,
  which days, which months, on or off.

**Rewards**: the same − / + on every reward's price, per son.

**Whole-son dial**: scale all of one son's chore pay up or down (e.g. +20% for a month
to build momentum). Compliments and bonuses aren't affected.

**Pay scale**: the coins-per-minute and the hardness multipliers behind the suggestions.

**Other settings**: screen-time limits and quiet hours, cash-out rate and weekly cap,
helpers and their limits, devices.
